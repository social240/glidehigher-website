THE DETAILS

What you might
want to know.

Does GlideHigher sell existing AI tools or develop custom solutions?+

Both. We help businesses select and implement established AI platforms and also develop custom AI applications, workflows, and integrations when specialized functionality is required.

How do I know which AI solution is right for my business?+

We begin by understanding your goals, current systems, budget, and operational challenges. We then recommend an approach based on the business requirements and available technology.

Can you integrate AI with our existing software?+

Depending on your systems, we can integrate AI with CRM platforms, communication tools, document repositories, business applications, and other software using supported APIs and integrations.

Do we need technical knowledge to work with GlideHigher?+

No. We translate business requirements into technical solutions and guide your team through implementation and adoption.

Can we start with one AI solution?+

Absolutely. Many businesses begin with one focused project, evaluate its results, and expand from there.

How much does an AI implementation cost?+

Pricing depends on the solution, integration requirements, customization, and ongoing support needs. After an initial discussion, we can outline the scope and pricing structure.

Are there ongoing subscription or maintenance fees?+

Some AI platforms require recurring software subscriptions. Custom solutions may involve hosting, usage-based services, maintenance, and support. Applicable fees are described in your project proposal.

Will we own our custom AI solution?+

Ownership, licensing, source-code access, and third-party technology rights are defined in the project agreement before development begins.

Does GlideHigher provide ongoing support?+

Yes. Support options may include troubleshooting, system maintenance, updates, training, and ongoing improvements, depending on the selected service agreement.

Can AI replace manual business processes?+

AI can automate many repetitive tasks, but not every process should run without human oversight. We identify where automation is appropriate and where review and approval should remain in place.
